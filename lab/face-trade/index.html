Face-to-face trade test

Checks whether two phones held screen to screen can read each other's codes with their front cameras.

  1. Open this page on both phones and turn the screen brightness up.
  2. Pick the same settings, then press Start on both.
  3. Hold the phones facing each other, 20 to 30 cm apart, tops together. You will hear a beep per step and three at the end.

Nothing leaves the phone: the camera is read in this page only.
