QModem lab

Checks whether two phones held screen to screen can move data through each other's front cameras, and how fast.

  1. Open this page on both phones and turn the screen brightness up.
  2. Press Start on both. The settings need not match: each phone chooses how it sends.
  3. Hold the phones facing each other, 20 to 30 cm apart, tops together. A short beep is an acknowledgement, a low one a stall, three at the end.
  4. When a phone has everything it shows a small completed card. Keep holding until both say Completed.

Nothing leaves the phone: the camera is read in this page only.
