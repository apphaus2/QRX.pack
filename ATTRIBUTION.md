# Attribution

## Original content (CC0 1.0)

The glyph documents, bodies' settings, minds and personas, `images/test-card.png`, `sounds/song.wav`,
`sounds/yip.wav`, `models/bush.glb`, `models/butterfly.glb`, `models/bowl.glb`, `models/bed.glb`, `models/fish.glb`,
`models/fish-blue.glb`, `models/trout.glb` and `models/tank.glb` were made for QRX and are dedicated to the public domain
under CC0 1.0 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). The sounds, the test card and every model are generated by the scripts in
`scripts/`; nothing in them is sampled or traced. (Quaternius' CC0
[Animated Fish Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/animatedfish.html) was looked at for the fish: its models have one
swim clip and no glTF of their own, so the fish here are written by hand like the others.)

Every creature under `species/` (its document, its model, its sounds, its prompt and its tricks) was made for QRX too
and is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. The models and the sounds are written by `species/build.py` from
the definitions and tables beside it (the sounds are synthesized in `species/lib/sounds.py`: tones and noise, no
recordings); nothing in them is sampled or traced, and the names and designs are original. The biome list uses the class codes and names of ESA
WorldCover (its product user manual); no WorldCover data is included.

Every bed and cue under `soundscapes/` was made for QRX and is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. They are
written by `soundscapes/build.py` from the recipes beside it: noise and tones with fixed seeds, no recordings, nothing
sampled. The biomes are ESA WorldCover's class codes and names, as for the species.
